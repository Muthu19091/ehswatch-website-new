About IRIS

IRIS (Intelligent Risk & Insight System) is EHSWatch's embedded AI layer — built into every workflow your safety team already uses. Six capabilities work together to surface hazards earlier, accelerate incident closure and turn raw safety data into actionable intelligence that used to take days to compile manually.

“IRIS doesn't replace your safety team's judgment — it sharpens it.”

Why Traditional EHS Systems Fall Short

Human attention, manual processes and scattered data create dangerous gaps.

Attention Constraints

Human attention misses hazards and hidden patterns buried in large volumes of safety data.

Manual Reporting Friction

Forms-based reporting discourages timely incident submissions, creating data gaps at the source.

Surface-Level Investigations

Investigations that stop at symptoms fail to uncover true root causes — letting repeat incidents happen.

Scattered Incident Records

Isolated records make it impossible to detect patterns or similarities across sites and time periods.

Manual Insight Compilation

Aggregating data into meaningful EHS insights is time-intensive and often done too late to act.

Reviewer Fatigue

Visual hazards in field photos are overlooked when reviewers are overwhelmed or fatigued.
