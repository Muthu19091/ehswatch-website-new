About the Summit

The HSE Intelligence Summit brings together industry leaders and HSE professionals to explore the evolving role of AI, data, and intelligent technologies in creating safer, more resilient, and sustainable organizations.

Through keynote insights, expert discussions, customer experiences, and live technology demonstrations, the summit will explore how organizations can move from traditional EHS management toward intelligent, proactive decision-making.

The summit will also feature the official reveal of EHSWatch AI: IRIS.
